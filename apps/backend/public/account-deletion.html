Delete your account

You can delete your MiniDrama account at any time from inside the app. Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Open MiniDrama and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be removed, then tap Continue.
  5. Enter your password to confirm. If you have been watching without an account, type DELETE instead.

What is deleted

Removed immediately and permanently

  • Your email address, phone number and password
  • Your profile, display name and preferences
  • Everything you have watched, and where you stopped in each episode
  • Your saved dramas
  • Every device you are signed in on is signed out

What is kept

Anonymous viewing statistics

  • Counts of how often episodes were played and how far into them people watched. Your account is unlinked from these before they are kept, so they cannot be traced back to you.
  • They are deleted entirely within 90 days.
  • They contain no email address, phone number, name or device identifier.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery period and no way to restore an account afterwards. You can sign up again with the same email address, but it will be a new account — nothing from the old one carries over.

If you cannot open the app

If you have lost access to your device or can no longer sign in, write to privacy@minidrama.app from the email address on the account and we will delete it for you.
